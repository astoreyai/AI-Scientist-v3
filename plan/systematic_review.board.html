Systematic review — atomic task graph

End-to-end systematic review (interventions, with notes for diagnostic and prognostic reviews) from question to publication. Reuses the scoping lanes for search, screening, writing and submission; adds dual extraction, risk of bias, meta-analysis or SWiM synthesis, and GRADE. An AI is one of the two independent reviewers at screening, extraction and risk of bias, with a human second reviewer and human resolution of every disagreement.
