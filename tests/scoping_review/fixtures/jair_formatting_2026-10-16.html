JAIR Formatting

Instructions for Formatting JAIR Articles

As of the spring of 2025 JAIR has moved to a new formatting style. After almost 35 years with the same format, the editors decided that it would be worthwhile to move to a more modern format. Specifically, we have adopted a format based on ACM's LaTex style, which is appropriate since JAIR now appears in the ACM library.

If your article is scheduled for publication in Voume 83 or a subsequent volume, or you are about to submit an article to the journal, you will be required to use the new JAIR LaTex style. The template can be found in the JAIR Author Kit. You can also find the template on Overleaf at https://www.overleaf.com/read/hycbzkdksrzz#8106d4. You can click "Menu" and then "Copy Project" to make your own project and start editing.
